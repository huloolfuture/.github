# Security Policy

We take the security of Hulool Future products and our students' data seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue. Email **admin@huloolfuture.sa** with:

- the affected product and URL,
- steps to reproduce,
- the impact you observed.

We acknowledge reports within **3 business days** and keep you updated until the issue is resolved. Please give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before any public disclosure.

## Scope

All products and domains operated by Hulool Future, including `huloolfuture.sa`.
